How to play

  • 🎮 2 players, 2 devices. One makes a room, the other joins with its 4-letter code.
  • 💡 Each round one player is the Clue-giver: they see a secret word and send a clue of max 3 words + max 3 emojis.
  • 🔍 The other player has 3 chances to guess it. Roles swap every round.
  • ⭐ Guess on 1st try = 3 pts, 2nd = 2 pts, 3rd = 1 pt. The clue-giver earns 0 pts if the guess is right, but 3 pts if the guesser fails all 3 tries (the answer is then revealed to both).
  • 🏆 First to 10 points wins the match!
